ecom-archive

ecom-archive is a private backup tool. It copies its owner's own data files into its owner's Google Drive, and reads them back when they are needed again.

It has one user: the person who runs it. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

  • It can see only the files it put in Google Drive itself, nothing else in the Drive.
  • It shares nothing with anyone.
